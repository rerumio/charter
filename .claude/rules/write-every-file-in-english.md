# Write every file here in English — this law is read worldwide

These texts are read by the platform's authors, in every country it reaches, and not by its
developers. Law written in the founder's own language quietly announces whose platform it is.
A repository half in one language and half in another cannot be quoted, translated, or amended
by anyone standing outside that half.

- **Every file, no exception.** The law, the README, notes to yourself, commit
  messages — all English. "It is only an internal note" is where the split always begins.
- **Draft in English, never carry a text across.** A clause thought out in another language and
  then translated keeps that language's sentence shapes and reads as a translation. Write it in
  English from the start, or rewrite it, but never convert it.
- **Keep one word per term.** Where a term is in use, use that exact word, never a synonym that
  happens to sit better in the sentence. Law that varies its vocabulary cannot be quoted against
  itself.
- **Conversation is not a file.** Talk with the human happens in whatever language they use.
  Nothing of that language reaches what gets written down.
- **The exception.** An external text held here — a licence, a statute, a demand received — stays
  verbatim in its original language, with the English beside it, never instead of it.
