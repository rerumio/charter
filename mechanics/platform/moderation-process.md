# Moderation

## TL;DR

Moderation is the enforcement of the platform's published rules against a post, a comment or an
account. A holder of `moderate` acts, every action names the rule it rests on, and every action
is a public record. Moderation never edits a text, never moves an address, and an upheld appeal
undoes it.

## Definition

Moderation is the enforcement of the platform's published rules against a post, a comment or an
account. Only a holder of `moderate` acts, within the scope their grant names.

An action rests on one rule, published before the act, and names it. The rules themselves stand
apart, in the moderation rules; this mechanic sets how an action is taken, not what the rules
forbid.

Every action, and every lifting of one, is a public record: the target, the rule, the actor, the
time.

An action is lifted the way it is taken: by a holder of `moderate`, naming its ground, as a public
record. An upheld appeal lifts an action; what it removed returns.

Moderation never edits a text and never moves its address.

## Particulars

- An action targets one post, one comment or one account.
- Nobody moderates their own post or their own comment.
- An appeal against an action is reviewed as appeals sets out.

## Implementation

Three actions, from lighter to heavier:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Delisting | The post leaves the catalog and every listing. It stays readable at its address. |
| Hiding | The text closes behind its record. The address keeps working and shows the record in its place. |
| Suspension | The account acts no more: it publishes nothing, comments on nothing, casts no vote. What it published stands, readable. |

A comment is hidden, never delisted; it stands in no listing to leave.

Which action each rule carries stands beside the rule, in the moderation rules.
