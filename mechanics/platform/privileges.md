# Access

## TL;DR

Access is the right to act on the platform's behalf: to admit a post, to remove one, to shape a
domain, to grant access itself. It is granted by a person, never earned, and every grant names a
scope and stands as a public record.

## Definition

Access is the right to act on the platform's behalf. It is granted by a person and never earned by
anything a person does, and it carries duty rather than advantage.

Access is held as sets. The sets are independent: each is granted, held and withdrawn on its own,
and holding one implies no other.

Access does one thing. It opens the actions of its set, within the scope its grant names, for as
long as that grant stands.

Every grant, and every withdrawal, is a public record.

## Particulars

- Every grant names a scope: one domain, or every domain. A set is defined once — scope lives in the
  grant and never in the name of the set.
- A grant over a domain covers its topics.
- Nobody grants what they do not hold, and nobody grants their own access.
- The platform's creator holds every set from the start, self-granted, as a public record; the
  separations of this file do not bind the creator.
- `appoint` is held alone. It never arrives together with another set.
- Every grant names who granted it, when, and until when. A grant that is not renewed lapses.
- The register of who holds what is open.

## Implementation

| Set | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `admit` | Decides admission to the catalog, and revokes it. |
| `taxonomy` | Creates topics; renames, merges and retires topics and keywords; moves a single post between domains, topics and keywords. |
| `domain` | Creates and shapes domains. |
| `collections` | Creates, shapes and retires collections, and adds and removes their posts. |
| `verify` | Confirms and revokes a qualification. |
| `medals` | Issues and annuls medals. |
| `moderate` | Removes by the rules, restores what an upheld appeal returns, and decides authorship claims. |
| `answer` | Answers an appeal. |
| `promote` | Raises and lowers a rank. |
| `appoint` | Grants and withdraws access sets. |
