# Admission to the catalog

## TL;DR

Admission is a post's right to stand in the catalog. A post earns it by its votes, arrives with it
on its author's standing, or receives it by decision — and a post without it is published all the
same, readable at its address. Only a locked post is admitted. Admission never removes anything,
and nothing is admitted by payment.

## Definition

Admission is a post's right to stand in the catalog. It settles one thing: whether the post is
listed. A post that is not admitted is published all the same — readable at its address.

Admission is held by the post, not by its author.

A post is admitted by any one of three routes: by its votes, by its author's standing, or by
decision of a holder of `admit` in the domain of the post. A holder of `admit` refuses admission,
or revokes admission already given. Each decision names the rule it
rests on and is a public record.

Whatever the route, only a locked post is admitted. How a post locks stands in post metadata.

Admission never removes a post, hides it, or moves its address. A post outside the catalog is a
post nobody listed, not a post anybody took away.

Nothing is admitted by payment.

## Particulars

- Only an admitted post stands in Trends or in a feed. An admitted post stands in Trends for its
  week from publication.
- Admission by votes follows on its own; nobody decides it.
- A post that has not yet been admitted has not been refused. Nothing is recorded until somebody
  decides something.
- Every figure below is published. A domain publishes its own figures or takes the platform's.
- The figures are reviewed on a published schedule and amended as the platform grows.

## Implementation

Three routes; the first to arrive admits the post.

| Route | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| By its votes | Votes on the post reach a weight of 100. |
| By its author | The author holds the rank of Established or above, or a reputation of 100 or more within the domain of the post. Their posts are admitted as they lock. |
| By decision | A holder of `admit` in the domain of the post admits it. |

What a vote weighs stands in voting. The ladder stands in ranks; the count stands in reputation.
