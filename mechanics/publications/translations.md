# Translations

## TL;DR

A translation carries a post's text into another language. Any account proposes one; the post's
authors approve or decline it. Nothing else puts a translation up.

## Definition

A translation carries a post's text into another language.

Any account proposes a translation. The authors of the post approve it or decline it. An approved
translation stands with the post, in its language.

## Particulars

- What a proposal counts for stands in temperature; what approved translations count for stands
  in medals.
