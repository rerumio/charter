# Accounts

## TL;DR

An account is how anyone acts on the platform, registered with an email. Its holder deletes it at
will; what it published stands. Deletion removes no text.

## Definition

An account is registered with an email and a password. Every account is of one kind — a user or
an organization; the kinds stand in organizations.

An account is deleted by its holder, at will. The account closes; its posts, comments and votes
stand. Deletion removes no text and moves no address.

## Particulars

- Registration confirms the email with a code sent to it.
- A nickname is set at registration and freely changed.
