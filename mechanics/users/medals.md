# Medals

## TL;DR

A medal is a mark of something an account did here, issued through the `medals` access against
conditions published in advance. Nothing buys one. Some medals raise the weight of their
holder's votes; every medal stands on their profile.

## Definition

A medal is a mark of something an account did here. A holder of `medals` issues it against
conditions published before it can be earned, and annuls it. Nothing buys one.

A medal does two things. It appears on its holder's profile, and some medals raise the weight of
their holder's votes. A medal grants no access and confers no rank.

A medal is permanent once issued. It falls away only where what it rests on falls away, or where it
was never earned.

## Particulars

- The conditions of a medal are not changed for a medal already in circulation. A changed condition
  makes a new medal.
- A medal obtained by fraud or issued in error is annulled. The record of issue and annulment is
  private, held for holders of `moderate`.
- A medal that rests on a verified qualification is annulled when that qualification is revoked.
- A medal bound to a domain raises weight only within that domain.

## Implementation

| What is marked | The medal |
| --- | --- |
| A verified qualification | A qualification the platform confirmed, bound to the domain it was verified in. |
| Time | One medal for each year of continued presence. |
| Taking part | A review round, a translation drive, a themed collection the platform ran. |
| A body of work | A stated count of admitted posts, approved translations, or appeals answered. |

Each medal that carries weight computes its own addition — a fixed amount, or a multiplier on a
stated measure — published with its conditions. Medals held together add together. How weight is
counted stands in voting.
