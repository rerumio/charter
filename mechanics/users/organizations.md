# Organizations

## TL;DR

An organization is an account of its own kind, registered as such at sign up. It publishes,
comments, votes and holds reputation as a user does. Its posts never stand without their human
authors.

## Definition

An organization is an account of its own kind. Every account is of one kind — a user or an
organization — chosen at registration. An organization is not created by another account; it is
registered as itself, and acts as itself.

An organization holds members. A member enters by an invitation, or by a request where the
organization allows requests.

A post of an organization always carries its human authors. How a post stands with its authors
stands in post metadata.

An organization publishes, comments, votes and holds reputation, rank and medals as a user
does. The ceiling of its vote stands in voting.

## Particulars

- The organization's profile holds what a user's profile holds.
- The organization chooses: join requests open, or entry by invitation only.
- A member leaves at will; the organization removes a member.

## Implementation

| Entry setting | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Requests open | Anyone sends a request; the organization decides it. Invitations work as well. |
| Invitations only | Entry is by invitation from the organization alone. |
