# Ranks

## TL;DR

A rank is an account's standing on the platform, conferred by hand and taken back by hand. It raises
the weight of every vote they cast, and above a published step it puts their posts into the catalog
without waiting for votes. It changes nothing else.

## Definition

A rank is an account's standing on the platform: how much of their work the platform has seen and
stands behind. A holder of `promote` confers it, and takes it back the same way.

A rank does two things. It raises the weight of every vote the account casts, and above a published
step it admits their posts to the catalog as they lock, without waiting for votes.

It does nothing else. A rank grants no access, opens no action, and never changes what an account may
read, what they may publish, or where their texts stand in any listing.

The ladder is walked one step at a time, up or down, and nothing is skipped. No rank is conferred
on grounds that were not published first.

## Particulars

- Nobody promotes themselves, and nobody promotes to their own rank or above it.
- Every promotion and every demotion names its reason and stands as a public record.
- The grounds on which each rank is conferred are published before anyone is conferred it.
- A rank does not lapse on its own. Absence is measured by temperature.

## Implementation

| Rank | Who holds it |
| --- | --- |
| Newcomer | Every account starts here. Nothing published yet, or nothing that has found readers. |
| Contributor | Publishes, and the posts are read. The first rank earned rather than held on arrival. |
| Established | Has a body of work in a domain. Other authors cite it, answer it and build on it. |
| Distinguished | Has kept that up over years, at a quality the platform names as its own standard. |
| Eminent | Has done so across a working life, and is known for it outside the platform. |

What each step adds to vote weight stands in voting. The rank above which an account's posts are
admitted stands in admission.
