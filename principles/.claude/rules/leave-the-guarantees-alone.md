# Leave the guarantees alone — amend one only when told to, by name

A guarantee is worth exactly as much as its resistance to being reworded. It is edited the
way it always is: not by anyone deciding to weaken it, but by someone tidying nearby text,
smoothing a sentence, making a clause "clearer" — and the promise quietly reads wider than it
did. Once that has happened even once, nobody outside believes the rest.

- **Touch one only on an instruction naming it.** Working in this folder is not permission.
  A request to fix the constitution's spacing is not a request to restate its clauses.
- **Add, never subtract.** A guarantee is never removed, never weakened, never reworded in
  the platform's favour, by no majority and under no circumstances.
- **Treat clarity edits as amendments.** If a clause can be read more broadly after the edit
  than before, that was a weakening, whatever the intent behind it.
- **Let the guarantee win the contradiction.** When a mechanic or a feature collides with a
  guarantee, the mechanic or the feature is wrong. Fix that side and leave this one standing.
- **Guard the terms as part of the text.** A term may be redefined freely — unless the
  redefinition changes what a guarantee means. Then it is an amendment and obeys this rule.
- **The exception.** A new guarantee may be added, and only added, through the amendment
  procedure the constitution names. Nothing here permits removing one.
