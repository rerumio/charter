# What the platform waits for

The texts the platform exists for. Nothing here admits a text, refuses one, or forbids one.

Every text has the same right to stand.

## Most

- A text that carries what its author knows: work done, a question gathered, a thing lived
  through.
- A text whose claims name what they rest on: a source, a method, or the case its author was in.
- A text that states what follows from it: what it concluded, what it taught, what it changed.
- A text about what did not work: a failed attempt, a negative result.

## Least

- A text that states what is not obvious and names nothing under it.
- An account of a case that leads nowhere.
- A text that circles a subject and settles nothing.
