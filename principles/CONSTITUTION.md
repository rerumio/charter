# Constitution 0.1.0

## Guarantees

1. **Reading is always free.** Every part of a published post is readable by anyone, without
   payment and without an account, at publication and afterwards. A post closes only under the
   published moderation rules; its address then serves the record of the closing.
2. **Money does not buy attention.** No payment, gift or exchange raises a text, an account or a
   domain in any listing, or lowers another. The platform sells no placement. Nothing bought
   grants an account a right another account lacks.
3. **Order follows published rules.** What stands in a listing and where it stands follow rules
   published in full, the same for every reader. Nothing a reader did here, other than voting
   and the settings they made themselves, is an input to any order. A reader's setting changes
   what that reader sees and nothing else.
4. **A name on a text means that person wrote it.** A name joins a text only by an act of the
   person it names. Every part a machine generated — text, image, narration or translation — is
   marked as machine-generated where it stands.
5. **Nothing disappears silently.** Every act of the platform against a post, a comment or an
   account — removal, hiding, delisting, refusal, revocation, suspension — enters a public
   record: what, when, under which rule, by whom. A removal compelled from outside names the
   compulsion; where law forbids naming its source, the record states that.
6. **Published text is editable for seven days and no longer.** An edited text is marked as
   edited. Once locked — by its authors, or when the window ends — its authors may only append
   to it, and an appended part never alters what stands above it.
7. **Every appeal is answered.** Anyone may write to the platform, at any time, suspended or
   not. An answer comes within thirty days and addresses what was asked. Appeal and answer are
   public; an appellant may close the contents of their own appeal — its fact, its date and the
   fact of an answer stay public. An appeal unanswered at the term is marked overdue, and the
   mark stays. Nobody answers an appeal against their own decision.
8. **Addresses never change.** A published text has one permanent address; a link to it always
   works while the platform stands. No address is reused. An address carries no domain and no
   topic — nothing a post is moved between changes it.
9. **Reading history belongs to the reader.** It is theirs to switch off, to erase, and alone
   to share. Unshared, it is shown to no one, sent nowhere, handed over to no one, and feeds no
   figure shown to anyone else. It is an input to no order but the reader's own.
10. **The archive is never closed.** Anyone downloads the whole public archive, at any time,
    without payment, without an account, and without a condition of the platform's own. Each
    text carries its author's licence.
11. **A locked text never leaves the platform.** It stands as it was when it locked, its addenda
    beside it. No act of its authors, no closing of an account and no departure removes it. It
    is removed only where law compels, and the removal enters the public record.
