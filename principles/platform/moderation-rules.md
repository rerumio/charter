# Moderation rules

The rules moderation enforces. A rule stands here before any action rests on it, and each rule
names the action it carries. The actions stand in the moderation mechanic.

## How a rule is read

A rule holds against what a text does, not against what it is about. Description, analysis,
history and testimony fall under no rule here.

Every rule is read by its own words and no wider. What no rule names is not forbidden.

An instruction is a text whose steps are sufficient to carry the act out.

Consent is the consent of the person the data or the depiction is of.

Where the age of a person depicted is not established, the depiction falls under R1.

Where one text breaches several rules, the heaviest action stands. A rule carrying two actions is
two actions, each its own record.

## The rules

| Rule | What it forbids | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | A sexual depiction of a minor, by any means made, drawn or generated. | Hiding and suspension |
| R2 | An intimate depiction of a person, published without that person's consent. | Hiding and suspension |
| R3 | An offer to buy or sell a person, a human organ, a weapon, a drug, a stolen credential, a forged document, or access to another's account. | Hiding and suspension |
| R4 | A call to kill or injure a named person or a group, a threat to do so, or a call to join or fund an armed attack. | Hiding and suspension |
| R5 | An instruction for an act of mass harm: a weapon of mass casualties, an explosive device, a poison, or an intrusion into a system people depend on. | Hiding and suspension |
| R6 | Code, or a link to it, published to damage a reader's device, take what is held on it, or take a credential by deceit. | Hiding and suspension |
| R7 | A demand for money, a credential or data on a false pretext. | Hiding and suspension |
| R8 | An account or a text presented as another person or a real organisation, or as holding a qualification, post or affiliation not held. | Hiding and suspension |
| R9 | Denial of a group's humanity, or a demand for its exclusion or elimination, by origin, race, language, sex, religion, disability or orientation. | Hiding |
| R10 | Another person's private data, published without their consent: where they live, their documents, their contacts, their workplace, or the tie between an account and their name. | Hiding |
| R11 | Repeated address to a person who has refused it, or a call for others to descend on a named person. | Hiding |
| R12 | Encouragement of suicide or of self-harm, or an instruction in its method. | Hiding |
| R13 | A recording of a real killing, torture or mutilation, standing without an account of what it shows. | Hiding |
| R14 | Content whose publication is a crime under the law the platform is subject to. | Hiding |
| R15 | Invented data, sources or quotations, presented as real. | Delisting |
| R16 | Another's text presented as one's own: a borrowed post that does not name its original. | Delisting |
| R17 | Machine-generated text standing unmarked under an author's name. | Delisting |
| R18 | Spam: a post or comment whose purpose is advertising or link-selling, not knowledge. | Delisting |
| R19 | Votes cast for payment or by arrangement, and accounts made to cast them. | Suspension |
| R20 | An account made by the holder of a suspended account while that suspension stands. | Suspension |
| R21 | Repeated breach of these rules by one account. | Suspension |
